How it works

This system uses a classical machine learning model (SVM, Random Forest or MLP) trained on the PlantVillage dataset to identify diseases of tomato, potato and pepper bell. The same results page appears for all three sources: top 3 matches, sample images and health tips.

🖼️ Image Source

  1. Choose a leaf photo.
  2. The model extracts shape and colour features.
  3. It compares them with the dataset and shows the top 3 diseases with confidence.

📝 Text Source

  1. Type the symptoms in English or Telugu.
  2. Your words are matched with disease symptom keywords.
  3. You get the matching diseases, images and tips. Mention the plant name for better matches.

🎤 Voice Source

  1. Pick English or Telugu and tap Start Voice.
  2. Your speech becomes text (Chrome or Edge).
  3. Tap Predict Disease to follow the same steps as text.
Supported plants: Tomato, Potato, Pepper bell. Other plants may give wrong answers, so low-confidence results show "Not sure".
