🖥️ About Classical ML

Using proven machine learning on a normal computer to detect plant diseases from images, text and voice. 🧱 Classical Basics 💡 Bit: the basic unit of information. It is either 0 or 1, never both. 🔍 Features: numbers taken from the leaf, like shape (HOG) and colour. The model learns from these. 📚 Learning: the model studies many labelled leaf images and learns which patterns mean which disease.

📈 SVM (Support Vector Machine)

  1. Draws the best boundary between disease classes
  2. Works well on small and medium data
  3. Trains fast and gives stable results

🌲 Random Forest

  1. Builds many decision trees
  2. Every tree votes, and the most voted disease wins
  3. Less overfitting, because many trees average out mistakes

MLP (Multi-Layer Perceptron)

  1. Layers of connected neurons
  2. Learns complex patterns from leaf features
  3. Improves by adjusting its weights during training

⚙️How Sources Works

  • 🖼️ Image Source Upload a leaf photo. Features (shape and colour) are extracted, and the trained model predicts the top 3 diseases with health tips.
  • ⌨️ Text Source Type the problem in English or Telugu. A keyword matcher compares your words with disease symptoms and shows the best matches with health tips.
  • 🎤 Voice Source Speak in English or Telugu. Your speech is converted to text, then matched like the Text Source, and the result is shown with health tips.
Note: This system only supports Tomato, Potato, Pepper bell because the model was trained only on these plants.Other plants may give wrong answers, so low-confidence results show "Not sure".
