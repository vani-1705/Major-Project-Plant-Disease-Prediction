About Quantum ML

Quantum Machine Learning (QML) combines the power of quantum computing with machine learning to solve complex problems faster and more accurately. It uses quantum principles like superposition, entanglement and parallelism to analyse data and make better predictions, especially in challenging real-world scenarios like plant disease detection.

⚛️ QSVM

Quantum Support Vector Machine uses a quantum kernel to find the best decision boundary between healthy and diseased leaves, improving accuracy and speed.

🔀 VQC

Variational Quantum Classifier is a hybrid quantum-classical approach. A parameterised quantum circuit is trained by a classical optimizer to classify the data efficiently.

🧠 QNN

Quantum Neural Network leverages quantum circuits as neural network layers to learn complex patterns in plant leaf data.
